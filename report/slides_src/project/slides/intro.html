What is vision-based adaptive signal control?

A junction repeatedly decides which approach gets green next, and for how long.

Fixed-time plans repeat the same timings. Adaptive control measures traffic and decides from the measurement.

Vision-based: a camera replaces loop detectors. YOLO detects vehicles, ByteTrack follows each one across frames.

A loop detector sees one spot. A camera sees the whole approach, so it can measure where the queue ends.

Bellevue 116th Ave NE / NE 12th St camera, this project's demo overlay

Explain the decision a signal makes. Point at the frame: boxes are YOLO detections, the numbers are ByteTrack IDs, each coloured region is an approach, and the red marks show where the measured queue ends. The key idea: a camera sees the whole road, not just a spot.
