Why measuring queues from video is hard

Perspective

Far vehicles are small; one pixel threshold for "stopped" is wrong near and far.

Recall at distance

YOLOv8m found 4 of about 9 queued cars on the far North leg.

Tracking noise

Box jitter looks like motion; an ID switch breaks a vehicle's history.

Closed loop

Filmed cars obey the real signal, not ours: video cannot answer "what if".

Over-saturation

Queues reach the end of the road; counting sensors saturate exactly there.

Fair comparison

Tuning on the reported data inflates results; settings must be fixed first.

Six challenges. The first three are computer-vision problems I had to solve in the measurement. The last three are evaluation problems: recorded video cannot show what a different signal would have done, which is why I use a simulator, and every setting is fixed before the final test.
