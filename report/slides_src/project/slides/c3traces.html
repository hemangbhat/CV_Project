Visual comparison: queues over time

Raza-style holds each green for its band: queues sit near the end of storage.

Capacity-aware MP re-decides every 5 s and switches constantly; lost yellow time makes queues longer.

Actuated and proposed serve short, queue-driven greens.

One seed fixed in advance (short-minor, 3000 veh/h). On it the proposed method is slightly worse than actuated; over 20 seeds it has 20% less spillback.

This plays the role of the face images in a super-resolution paper: what the methods do, not just their averages. I chose the seed before looking, and on this seed my method is slightly worse; the averages over 20 seeds are what count.
