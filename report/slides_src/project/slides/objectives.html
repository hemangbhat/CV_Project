6 · Objectives

Four objectives

1

Design a robust spatial queue measurement from YOLO + ByteTrack: queue tail X and its 5 s projection S.

2

Test X and S in the selection score of a Raza-style controller (study 1).

3

Design a storage-aware score and storage protection after Mohajerpoor et al.; test against literature baselines over graded demand (study 2).

4

Measure robustness to camera errors: missed far vehicles and position jitter.

Objective 3 is the main enhancement. Objective 2 is the first attempt, which did not work; I keep it because it explains why objective 3 is designed the way it is.
