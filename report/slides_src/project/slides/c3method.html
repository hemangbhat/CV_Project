Proposed: scoreᵢ = Dᵢ + λ ( 1/(α − Sᵢ) − 1/α ) λ=1, α=1.1 (9)

Dᵢ Raza's PCE count, one common normaliser: storage-blind, as in the paper

Sᵢ camera queue tail as a share of this road's storage, projected 5 s: storage-aware

Eq. (9) Mohajerpoor's reciprocal penalty: ≈ 0 for short queues, steep near the end of the road

Eq. (10) their constraint xᵢ ≤ βLᵢ, applied online; greens are otherwise actuated, 10–60 s

The proposed equation is almost the same as Raza's, except for one storage term, plus one rule for ending a green. Both come from Mohajerpoor et al., but where they estimate the queue with a shockwave model, I measure it with the camera. Lambda, beta and the 20-second guard were chosen on validation seeds by a rule written down in advance.
