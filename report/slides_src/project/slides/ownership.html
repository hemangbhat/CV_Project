C3 · What is borrowed and what is mine

Mohajerpoor models the queue; I measure it

Borrowed

  • YOLOv8 detection, ByteTrack tracking
  • Raza's PCE density score and starvation guard
  • Mohajerpoor's storage constraint x ≤ βL and the shape of the reciprocal penalty
  • Actuated control and max pressure, as baselines

Mine

  • Queue axis, perspective-normalised stopped test, queue tail X and risk S
  • Measuring the queue position that FASC estimates with a shockwave model
  • An online rule inside Raza's controller instead of a cycle optimiser; the 20 s guard
  • Both studies, the camera-vs-count control, the noise test, pre-registration
Say this before anyone asks. The formula's shape is Mohajerpoor's and I cite it. What is mine is the vision measurement that replaces their model, the way it is built into a real-time camera controller, and the experiments that show where it helps and where it fails. The department's T-DLcR is built the same way: LcR plus a borrowed noise filter, adapted and tested.
