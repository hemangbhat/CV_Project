Relevant methods, their equations and limitations

Raza et al. (base)

Dᵢ = Σ nᵢ,c · PCE_c (3)
p = argmax Dᵢ
g = band(Dₚ)

Limitation: 10 cars on a 60 m road and on a 150 m road look the same; a green never ends early.

Capacity-aware MP

p = argmax nᵢ / Cᵢ (4)
re-decided every 5 s

Limitation: reacts to every small change; under heavy demand it switches often and loses time to yellows.

FASC (Mohajerpoor)

xₚ ≤ βₚ Λₚ (Eq. 9)
+ Σ Λₚ / (αₚΛₚ − δₚ) (5)

Limitation: queue position δₚ comes from a shockwave model and predicted demand, not a measurement.

Three equations to remember. Raza's is a count. Capacity-aware max pressure divides the count by the road's capacity. FASC constrains the queue to its link length and adds a penalty that explodes as the queue nears the end of the road. My method takes FASC's penalty and constraint, and feeds them a camera measurement.
