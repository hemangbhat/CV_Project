Key takeaways and future work

Next steps follow from the limits

  • Regime switch: detect from the camera when every approach is near full at once (FASC's queue-formation period) and relax β automatically.
  • Network test: an isolated junction under-values spillback; a two-junction SUMO corridor would measure the upstream blocking it causes.
  • Detector recall at distance is the measurement's binding limit; camera errors erode the near-capacity benefit on short roads. A larger model or tiling on far approaches comes first.
  • Fair reporting: capacity-aware max pressure is the best method at light demand, and actuated timing explains most of the gain over Raza.
Each future step answers one limitation from the results. The first one, a regime switch, is the direct fix for the failure beyond capacity and follows Mohajerpoor et al.'s own advice.
