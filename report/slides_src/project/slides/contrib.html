What the enhancement adds, and where it stops

  • A camera-measured version of Mohajerpoor et al.'s storage penalty and constraint: one term added to Raza's equation plus one timing rule.
  • Near capacity: 20–26% less local spillback than actuated control, with no significant change in delay.
  • On a short road, the measured queue tail beats a stopped-vehicle count (−115 s blocked entry): where measuring where the queue is pays off.
  • Against the Raza-style base: 34–87 s less delay in 9 of 10 conditions, mostly from actuated timing (shown by the ablation).
  • Beyond capacity, storage protection fails (delay about doubles): the regime Mohajerpoor et al. say needs the constraint relaxed.
Five points, in order of importance. Say the last one plainly: a controller that protects storage when spillback cannot be avoided only adds switching. That is a limitation and the next step, not something to hide.
