4 · Problem formulation

Measure where the queue ends, then control with it

X̂ᵢ(t) = h(Iₜ, Iₜ₋₁, … ; θ) ≈ xᵢ(t) / Lᵢ (1)

(pₖ, gₖ) = π( X̂(t), D̂(t), … ) (2)

minimise Σᵥ delayᵥ s.t. xᵢ(t) ≤ βᵢ · Lᵢ , g_min ≤ gₖ ≤ g_max

xᵢ queue-tail distance of approach i; Lᵢ its storage length

h the vision pipeline (detect, track, stopped test, tail search)

pₖ, gₖ approach served in green k and its duration

xᵢ ≤ βᵢLᵢ spillback avoidance (Mohajerpoor et al. 2023, Eq. 9)

Equation 1: the camera estimates where each queue ends, as a share of that road's storage. Equation 2: the controller picks the next approach and its green time. The goal is low total delay while keeping each queue inside its road; that constraint is taken directly from Mohajerpoor et al. The question: can a camera supply x, and does using it help?
