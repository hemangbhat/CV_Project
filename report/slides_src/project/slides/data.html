8 · Data, testbed and evaluation measures

Real video for measurement, simulation for control

Video

City of Bellevue Traffic Video Dataset; 116th Ave NE / NE 12th St; clips of 107, 240 and 240 s at 30 fps.

SUMO testbed

Simulation of Urban MObility. 4-arm junction, 2 lanes, 3 s yellow. A virtual camera computes D, Q, X, S with the video definitions; a noise mode drops 30% of far vehicles, 2 m jitter.

Measures

Mean delay per vehicle (s). Blocked-entry seconds: local spillback. 95% CIs over 20 paired seeds. Protocol committed before the test seeds.

Video answers "does the measurement work". Simulation answers "does control improve", because simulated cars respond to my signal. Like PSNR and SSIM in super-resolution, delay and spillback are the two measures. Every free setting was chosen on separate validation seeds before the final test.
