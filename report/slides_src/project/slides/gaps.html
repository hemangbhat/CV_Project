Research gaps and motivation

Cameras count; spillback control has to guess

Research gaps

  • Vision controllers (Raza) measure counts: blind to where the queue is relative to the end of the road.
  • Spillback-aware methods (FASC, Wei) need the queue position but estimate it from models.
  • Counts saturate at the stop-line zone: once full, a count forecast reads "steady" however fast the queue grows.

Motivation

  • A camera already sees the whole approach: it can measure what FASC must model.
  • Used the way the literature says it matters, as a storage constraint, it might cut spillback on short roads at no delay cost.
  • Whether it does is empirical, and recorded video cannot answer it.
The gap in one sentence: the controllers that have a camera only count, and the controllers that need queue position have no camera. Motivation: put the two together and test it honestly.
